About me and my brand

Hello, my name is Ariel Trapp and I am the owner and creator of Pressed by Starr. I am a small business owner who specializes in creating custom prints on cards, tote bags, and other personalized items. My passion for handmade art has led me to start this business, where I can share my love for art with others. Each piece is carefully crafted using wood cutting tools, lino or a slab of wood, making them unique and one-of-a-kind.
